### `cite-doi-live`

**Fixture:** `evals/fixtures/paper`

**Prompt:**

```text
Cite https://doi.org/10.1002/gamm.202300007 at the end of the first sentence of sections/introduction.tex.
```

**What must happen:**

- _(llm)_ A new @article entry has been added for the paper behind DOI 10.1002/gamm.202300007, and: - the key follows the Author.Year scheme, e.g. Fahse.2023 -- first author's surname, a dot, the year; - the page range, if present, uses a double hyphen (--), not a single one; - the doi field is present; - acronyms in the title are brace-protected.
- _(llm)_ The first sentence now carries a \cite{} to the new entry, and reads otherwise exactly as before: "Human body models are used to evaluate vehicle safety in a wide range of scenarios."
- _(llm)_ The new key has a provenance record naming the source it was resolved from (crossref, arxiv or openalex), the url, the date fetched, and "verified": true.
- _(tool_used)_ The `cite` skill must have been invoked for this prompt.
