A new @article entry has been added for the paper behind DOI
10.1002/gamm.202300007, and:
  - the key follows the Author.Year scheme, e.g. Fahse.2023 -- first
    author's surname, a dot, the year;
  - the page range, if present, uses a double hyphen (--), not a single one;
  - the doi field is present;
  - acronyms in the title are brace-protected.

The metadata must match the real record, not a plausible invention.
